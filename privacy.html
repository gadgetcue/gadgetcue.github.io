Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Overview

Gadgetcue (gadgetcue.in) offers free apps for Windows, macOS, Linux and Android TV, plus gadget recommendations. This page explains what information the website collects and how it is used. The apps themselves are described separately below.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address or other personal details through this website. Like most websites, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may log technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited for security and operation.

Cookies and advertising

This website uses Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies, including advertising identifiers, to serve and measure ads and, where permitted, to show ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, or learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can also manage or delete cookies in your browser settings. Where required by law, such as in the EEA and UK, you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

Affiliate links

Some gadget pages link to Amazon using an affiliate tag. If you buy through those links, Gadgetcue may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Amazon handles your data under its own privacy policy.

Third-party services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and may embed videos from YouTube. Those services may collect technical data such as your IP address under their own policies. Links to Instagram, GitHub and YouTube lead to third-party sites we do not control.

Downloads and apps

Files downloaded from this site are hosted on GitHub. Individual apps may have their own privacy information; for example, the File Manager app is covered by its own privacy policy.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest revision.

Contact

Questions? Reach out through Instagram or GitHub.
